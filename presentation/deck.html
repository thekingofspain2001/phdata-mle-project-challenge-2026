Problem

A seller's listing is missing the bathroom count or the plot size. The API rejects the whole request. No price comes back.

Impact

Up to 10–20% of live requests stall. The agent phones back, the seller waits, the valuation queue stops.

Cost

Every rejected listing is a conversation restarted and a deal delayed. Coverage, not accuracy, is what leaks.

Summary: similar homes win

Average and middle value give every blank the same city-wide guess. Similar homes adapt to the house — price-moving fields [knn.range] than averages.

Problem

Every request reloads the model, the maps, and the comparables from disk. Under load, that queues up.

Impact

Agents wait on every valuation. Peak traffic makes every answer slower.

Savings

Load once when the service wakes, serve from memory. Typical 56 ms down to 9 ms. Slow tail 79 ms down to 13 ms.
